My story

I'm an Aerospace Engineer turned Data Science enthusiast, with a background in engineering, industrial automation and problem-solving.

My journey into programming began during my Master's studies at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, where I used MATLAB to tackle aerodynamic and structural engineering challenges. My master's thesis included a preliminary structural analysis of an aircraft landing gear.

I moved to Poland in 2020 and, in 2021, I started to work in the medical industry, where I've continued exploring ways to make processes more efficient, including developing automation solutions in Visual Basic for orders entry. Along the way, my curiosity for Python grew into a passion for data science, leading me to complete the IBM Data Science Professional Certificate in 2024 and the HACK A BOSS Data Science & AI Bootcamp in 2025.

Today, I'm focused on continuous learning, building practical data projects and exploring the possibilities of AI to solve real-world problems.
